FilesDevxyz Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

What we store

When you upload a file, we store:

  • The file itself, its original filename, size, and upload time
  • A SHA-256 hash of your link password, only if you set one (we never store the password itself)
  • An expiry timestamp, only if you choose a link expiry

There are no accounts. The "My files" list on the upload page is kept in your own browser's localStorage — it never leaves your device.

Automatic deletion

Every file is automatically and permanently deleted 7 days after upload. Deleted files cannot be recovered. Total storage is capped at 9GB — uploads stop before the cap is exceeded.

Who can see your files

Files are private by default. They are only reachable through their unguessable 8-character link (/f/xxxxxxxx). Anyone you share the link with can download the file. If you set a password, the file additionally requires it. Do not share links to files you want to keep private.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your files or metadata with third parties.
  • We don't scan file contents for advertising or profiling.
  • We don't require personal information to use the service.

Infrastructure logs

Our hosting providers (Vercel and Cloudflare) keep standard operational logs (e.g. request timestamps, IP addresses) for security and reliability. We do not combine these logs with your files.

Abuse

Files reported as illegal, malware, or abusive may be reviewed and removed. To report abuse, contact businessdevgithubjepong@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
